# Mac street power artwork provenance

Created October 6, 2026 for the authorized Mac power-kit implementation. These are selected native artwork and measured registrations for the integration candidate; final gameplay, hosted release and physical-device acceptance are separate.

## Generation and style references

Two independent built-in `image_gen.imagegen` calls used `transparent_background: true`. The inspected local style references were `assets/mac-city-review/night-market-v1.png` and `assets/mac-city-review/relay-canal-v1.png`: painted dark ink outlines, worn timber and metal, warm amber and cyan light. They were inspected before prompt authoring; no reference image pixels were composited into the new sheets. Root reviewed both resulting atlases as readable and cohesive for the integration candidate.

Selected output bytes were copied directly from the built-in generator's output directory. No PNG resize, recolor, alpha cleanup, crop, repaint or recomposition was performed. Python/Pillow was used only to read RGBA pixels and calculate source-rectangle metadata. The requested prompt canvas was 2048 square; the tool delivered native **1254 × 1254** RGBA PNGs, and all registrations use those actual dimensions.

## Native registration and verification

The asset directory contains exactly the two selected PNG sheets and their JSON registry. Each of the 18 cells has a disjoint integer native source rectangle, a finite local pivot and a positive world display height. Every visible alpha > 8 pixel from both complete sheets is accounted for by the registered rectangles. Each measured silhouette has **eight native pixels of visible-ink clearance** on every crop edge. RGBA transparency, native dimensions, exact file hashes, complete occupancy, disjointness, crop bounds, ground pivots and prop-family scale were independently checked. Tiny alpha values at or below 8 are retained in the original generated bytes; no cleanup or claim that every gutter pixel has alpha zero is made.

Prop pivots are bottom-center at the measured visible ground boundary. Impact burst pivots are near their directional origin; heavy splashes use their center; flattened floor marks use their shallow-plane center. All floor marks draw at 35 world pixels of crop height and retain their wider-than-tall source proportions. Pickup height is 42; compact impacts are 80; heavy splashes are 140.

Each damage-state family uses one native pixel scale. With a 260-world-pixel actor reference, intact crate visible height is 120, stall canopy visible height is 240 and relay visible height is 220. The registry derives each sibling `displayHeight = source.height × familyPixelScale`; the rendered crop therefore uses the same scale in intact, cracked and collapsed states. Broken piles remain lower than intact objects.

## Exact generation prompts

### Props atlas

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent native game sprite atlas for the BARCODE System Override Mac street chapter. Create ONE 2048 by 2048 square RGBA sprite sheet, genuine fully transparent background, with EXACTLY NINE completely separate painted objects laid out as a clean 3 by 3 grid. No grid lines or labels. Each grid cell has a generous empty transparent border and at least 80 pixels of transparent gutter between all objects and the sheet edges; all debris stays within its own cell, nothing clips or overlaps. Camera: fixed side-on beat-em-up street view with a little top and right-face perspective, coherent with street architecture. Rich painterly ink-comic style: dark indigo outlines and cross-hatching, worn tactile surfaces, thin warm amber and cyan rim light, gritty night-market cyberpunk city, grounded and recognizable, no glossy 3D rendering or pixel art. Make the state variants clearly the SAME object in the SAME camera, size, colors and resting position.
Row 1, left to right: (1) sturdy intact waist-low wooden shipping crate, warm brown boards, dark teal steel corners, diagonal bracing, closed; (2) that SAME crate cracked, split front boards and loosened lid but still standing; (3) that SAME crate smashed flat into a compact pile of recognizable broken boards, bent teal corners and small splinters, low to the ground.
Row 2, left to right: (4) compact freestanding night-market vendor stall, weathered timber counter, dark teal fabric canopy on short posts, small baskets of green and orange produce, no people; (5) the SAME stall damaged, cracked counter and one sagging canopy corner, produce scattered only within cell; (6) the SAME stall destroyed into a compact low pile of timber, torn teal awning and scattered produce, visibly fully broken.
Row 3, left to right: (7) a compact vertical street broadcast relay cabinet, worn dark blue-gray metal, vent slats, bolts, unmarked controls, tiny dull red unlit status lamp, closed cabinet, powered OFF; (8) the SAME relay cabinet powered ON, exact identical silhouette and placement, cyan/teal illuminated indicator and modest clean cyan light in its vents, no external beams; (9) small collectible health pickup: a neat red-and-cream medical refill pouch with a simple white heart emblem, short cyan rim glow tightly around the pouch, no words, a clear silhouette.
Avoid text, numbers, logos, watermarks, characters, hands, background scenery, floor tiles, fake checkerboards, drop shadows detached from the object, opaque rectangles, cropping, mirrored different-state geometry. Whole objects with readable painted detail; true native alpha outside the nine objects.
```

No labels or grid lines. Generous fully transparent borders and at least 100 pixels of clear gutter between every effect and each image edge. No particle or drop clips or crosses its own cell.
