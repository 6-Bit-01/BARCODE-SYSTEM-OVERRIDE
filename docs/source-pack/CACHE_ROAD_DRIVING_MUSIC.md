# Cache Road — Music Earned by Driving

## What changed

The owner heard x3/x4 through most of the drive and a hit that merely softened the same song. This was reproducible. In a bar 21 traffic stretch, steering through all four lanes prepared the same upcoming four bars in 2.95 seconds without a hit. The former E/RB action could stamp all four lanes even faster. The after-hit bed kept drums, quiet Drive and quiet Flow; across eight sampled four-bar passages, its RMS was only about 2–3.5 dB below the full five-part mix. The bus stutter returned in 0.62 seconds.

This is a mechanical revision to the previously approved free four-bar locks. No part is rejected for a claimed quiet section, and Zone is still a reward rather than the price of music. The existing lane names and five owner MP3s are unchanged.

| Driving action | Music that follows |
| --- | --- |
| Center in a lane for 0.5 seconds | Its part is ready and previews on the next beat while centered. The hold by itself does not fill the stack. |
| Clean pass beside traffic after preparing the lane | The part stays audible through the current four-bar section, even after steering away. |
| Tight escape from traffic after preparing its lane | The part stays through the current section and the next aligned four bars. The existing cut also charges Zone. |
| E/RB | Instantly seals the current remainder and next four bars, once in each four-bar section. The HUD says when it has been used. |
| Traffic hit | Active, queued and prepared parts clear; the bus drops out briefly, returns on a beat, and the drums play alone until a new part is earned. The sources remain synchronized. |

The projected road paint appears for music actually sealed, not for a mere hold. The dashboard marks a prepared lane as `PASS`. A normal pass can seal the current section; a later tight escape in that same prepared lane can still carry it forward. The stack still reaches x4, but its parts must come from a deliberate sequence of traffic passes and/or the one immediate E/RB use. Repeated button presses and a safe lane sweep no longer make an automatic four-part mix.

## Verification and limits

The production harness checks the once-per-section button, intro and chorus captures with the actual recorded-role mapping, a safe four-lane sweep that does not become x4, and a planned traffic route that does reach x4 without invulnerability. It also covers a real close cut, Zone timing, hit recovery mix, paired gate reward suppression, the 100-bar drive, Echo exit, old saves and no Level 2 award. The browser gate decodes the five MP3s and checks synchronized starts and nonzero output. Automated steering proves reachability, not how often a human will earn x3/x4. The owner must judge driving and sound in merged Makko, including the weight of the beat-aligned hit break and whether the new skill rule feels fair on a physical controller.
