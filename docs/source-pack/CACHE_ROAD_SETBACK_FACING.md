# Cache Road exterior lots and road-facing fronts — September 25, 2026

The owner liked the larger place scale in merged #124 but observed that the
painted buildings and lots were placed over the sidewalk. Some houses showed
their backs to the road. Keep the enlarged individual art and curved road,
while moving the places into the landscape beyond the outside sidewalk edge.

## Placement

At each place's own world distance, project the outer sidewalk edge from the
same curved centerline as the road. Set its center one half of its displayed
width plus a growing gap farther outward. The painted image's innermost bound
is therefore beyond the walkway on both sides; the gap and whole image grow
together as the place approaches. Apply this to the quieter second row as
well. Move parcel foundations and the low kiosks, fences, foliage and wet
paving outside the walkway. Pedestrians, slab joints and streetlights remain
on it, and the streetlight art and size formula do not change.

The source frontage is on the left for nine of the ten images. The left road
mirrors them so the entrance is on their roadward edge; the right road shows
them unmirrored. The repair garage's large bay is on its right, so its facing
is reversed. A horizontal mirror changes the oblique painted yaw without
turning a roof upside down or reshaping the art.

The width-relative offset makes a near place leave the *outer* screen edge
earlier than it did when it occupied the sidewalk. It is still drawn as it
passes; its position and scale remain functions of one road-world distance.
An empty visible sidewalk is the intended separation from the landscape.

## Review and limits

The production draw test records the renderer's actual curved outer sidewalk
paths. It checks visible place bounding boxes against those paths at opening,
approach and a later district, plus road-facing flip rules and the market's
natural exit. Scripted seven-chapter and continuous native renders permit a
visual check at various bends. They are not a Makko playtest or a judgment of
how the roadside feels with the owner's music. The owner should inspect both
sides at normal size, especially the house entrance, garage bay, market
storefront, open lot gates, near-screen passage and sidewalk clearance.
Report an exact road position and frame if any painted footprint still seems
to intrude or a frontage appears backward.
