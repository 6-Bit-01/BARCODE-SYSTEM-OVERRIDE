## Level 1 polish after the first finishable encounter

Teach one threat/counter per encounter, then combine them. Show separate timing-miss/no-target/hit outcomes. Add selected musical enemy telegraphs, preserving immediate movement. Compare enemy rendering/contact before changing margins. Escalate the Jammer's presentation over its existing sixteen hits. Author the three Level 1 lore records and connect their persistent IDs as part of M2.

Do not turn this list into a prerequisite for every boss test. Fix a reproduced regression as part of the current milestone; keep other polish bounded so the project advances.

## Scope and asset discipline

Aim for seven polished compact levels. Earlier v4 first-clear estimates of roughly 12–18 minutes for action/puzzle/racing stages, 20–30 minutes for the party-RPG stage and 30–45 minutes for the finale are provisional budgeting targets, not promises or acceptance gates. Playtests determine actual length.

Every new genre gets a roughly five-minute playable proof before a large asset request: controls, camera, collision, one threat, one musical relationship, pause/exit and save-return. Start the Sample stage with 6–8 families, one small settlement and one route/battle loop. Expand only if that loop justifies it.

Build on the current engine and Makko pipeline. Introduce reusable infrastructure when the next consumer needs it, not as an unrelated rewrite. Asset requests specify exact size, anchor, animations, purpose and acceptance rather than asking the owner to guess what to draw.
