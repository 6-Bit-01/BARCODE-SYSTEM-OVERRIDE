# Cache Back mirror expression sheet

`cache-back-mirror-expressions.webp` is a 3 × 3 atlas of seven 512 × 512 cells.
Reading order: calm, focused, Turbo/Zone, close pass, impact brace, low signal,
rear check. The last two cells are empty. During a hit, the brace comes first;
the brief rear check follows while damage protection is still active.
The RGBA source PNG is kept under `sources/`; its navy background is transparent
outside the character, while his face and mask remain opaque.

The first sheet was generated from the owner's Cache Back model
`openart-image_sUa578AT_1765587750737_raw(1).png`, but cropped half his head
off the left edge. The replacement uses the owner's model as the identity
reference for a complete head in each cell: both brown eyes, rectangular
glasses, angular mask, cap with its fabric side flaps, collar and yellow jacket.
Five everyday expressions face the road. A separate impact brace has one eye
squeezed shut while the other stays forward; only the following recovery frame
checks the rear of the mirror. The model reference remains in the owner's files.

The runtime crops the eye/upper-mask band from each complete portrait into
the mirror with normal opaque compositing. The road remains visible around
the silhouette and the mirror's glare stays above it. The reflection is
decorative and never communicates actual traffic danger.
